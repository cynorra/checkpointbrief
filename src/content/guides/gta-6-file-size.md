**Rockstar has not published an official file size for GTA 6.** The PlayStation Store listing doesn't show one yet, and the Xbox listing shows only a placeholder of a few hundred megabytes, not the real game ([GTA Intel's roundup of the store listings](https://gtaintel.com/news/gta-6-preload-file-size-launch-times)). Most credible estimates land somewhere above 100 GB. To be safe, plan to have **at least 150 GB free** before pre-loading starts on November 12.

## Ignore the viral numbers

You've probably seen tables claiming 150, 200 or even 750 GB. None of them come from Rockstar or a store listing. One widely shared figure came from a parody account, and a supposed "GTA 6 download" passed around online turned out to be mostly padding bundled with malware. Until a store lists a size, any exact number is a guess.

The **512 GB Xbox Series S** is the tightest fit. A game of 150 GB would take up more than 40% of its usable space.

## How to check your free space

- **PS5:** Settings → Storage → Console Storage. It shows used and free space, and lets you sort games by size.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** Settings → System → Storage devices, or press the Xbox button and go to My games & apps → Manage.

## The quickest ways to make room

1. **Delete games you've finished.** Your saves usually stay on the console or in the cloud, and you can redownload games any time.
2. **Clear captures.** Video clips add up fast, especially at 4K.
3. **Move older games to a USB drive.** Both consoles can store current-generation games on a USB drive, and play older generation games from it, which frees the internal drive.
4. **Add storage** if you're always full. A PS5 takes a standard M.2 SSD; an Xbox uses an official expansion card.
