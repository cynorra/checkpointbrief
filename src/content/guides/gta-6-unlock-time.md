**GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in every region, on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.** That comes straight from [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o/grand-theft-auto-vi-regional-pre-load-and-release-times-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-series-x-or-s) (article last updated November 3, 2026): "Grand Theft Auto VI will release midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions for both PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X|S."

In plain terms, the game does not unlock everywhere at the same instant. It rolls around the world, one time zone after another, as each place reaches 12:00 a.m. on November 19. New Zealand goes first. Hawaii goes last, about 23 hours later.

The conversion is arithmetic on Rockstar's rule, not a separate announcement for each country. The rule itself is official; the table is ours. If a country changes its clocks or Rockstar edits its article, we update this page.

## What this means in the US and Canada

- **East Coast (Eastern):** 12:00 a.m. on Thursday, November 19, the night of Wednesday into Thursday.
- **Central:** 12:00 a.m. Thursday, which is 1:00 a.m. Eastern.
- **Mountain:** 12:00 a.m. Thursday, 2:00 a.m. Eastern.
- **West Coast (Pacific):** 12:00 a.m. Thursday, 3:00 a.m. Eastern. Pacific players wait the longest in the continental US, but they still get the game at midnight on their own clock, not at 9 p.m. the night before.

An older version of this guide said that all US time zones would unlock together at midnight Eastern, which would have put West Coast players in on Wednesday evening. Rockstar's official wording ("midnight local time ... in all regions") contradicts that, so we removed it.

## Is there an official time for each country?

Rockstar's support article gives the rule, not a country-by-country list. Store pages may show a countdown in your own time zone, and that is the number to trust on the day. Platform stores can behave differently from the rule on rare occasions, so check the countdown on your console's home screen rather than relying on any website, including this one.

## Pre-load, early access and region tricks

Pre-loading is different from unlocking. Pre-loading opened on **November 10, 2026**: on PS5 at midnight local time in each time zone, and on Xbox Series X|S at 12 a.m. GMT worldwide ([Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/7nPqOA2uCbpEQHXx7KVrYd/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-faq)). It only puts the files on your console. It does not let you play early. See the [pre-load guide](/guides/gta-6-pre-load/) for the steps.

People often ask whether changing your console's region or time zone would unlock the game earlier, because New Zealand unlocks first. Rockstar has not said anything about this, and no edition includes early access. On PS5, digital licences are tied to the PlayStation Store region of your account, so changing settings may do nothing, and opening a second account in another region means buying in that region's store and can cost you your pre-order bonuses. We don't recommend it.

## If the game stays locked at midnight

Millions of consoles check their licence at the same minute, so a short delay is normal.

1. **Wait one to two minutes.** Don't delete or reinstall the game.
2. **PS5:** go to **Settings → Users and Accounts → Other → Restore Licenses** and select **Restore**.
3. **Xbox:** hold the power button for 10 seconds until the console is fully off, unplug it for 30 seconds, and start it again.
4. **Both:** check that you're online and signed in to the account that holds the game, and that the console has finished any pending update.

For outages during launch week, see [PlayStation Network status](https://status.playstation.com/), [Xbox status](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/xbox-live-status) and [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/).

## Get ready before midnight

- **Pre-load first.** If you pre-ordered digitally, the download has been available since November 10, so on launch night you can play at once. The install needs up to 212 GB of space, per [Rockstar](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/7nPqOA2uCbpEQHXx7KVrYd/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-faq).
- **Physical copies:** the box holds a code, not a disc, and reaches retailers from November 12. Rockstar says codes can be used as soon as you receive them ([details](/guides/gta-6-physical-edition-no-disc/)).
- **Update early.** Turn the console on a few hours before your unlock time and let it install any update.
- **Plan around your own clock.** If midnight is a work night for you, remember the game stays installed and ready, so there's no rush to play at 12:00 a.m. sharp.
