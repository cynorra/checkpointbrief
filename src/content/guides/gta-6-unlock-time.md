**Rockstar hasn't announced an official unlock time for GTA 6, only the date: November 19, 2026.** PlayStation Store data examined by fans and reported by several outlets suggests each region unlocks at midnight in its main time zone, which for the US would be **midnight Eastern Time** on November 19 ([GTA Intel's summary](https://gtaintel.com/news/gta-6-preload-file-size-launch-times)). That's reported, not announced, and it can change.

Midnight Eastern on November 19 is 05:00 UTC. This table assumes the reported store timing holds. If Rockstar announces something different, we'll change it.

## Why "midnight" is complicated

A staggered unlock means each region starts at its own midnight. The reported PlayStation data shows roughly fifteen regional unlock times spread across about nineteen hours, starting with the earliest time zones on November 18. Microsoft hasn't published an equivalent breakdown for Xbox. Usually the two stores line up closely, but not always to the minute.

## Can you play earlier by changing region?

Don't rely on it. Your purchase is tied to your account's region, and unlock times follow the store you bought from. Changing your console's time zone doesn't change when a game unlocks.

## Be ready before it unlocks

1. **Pre-load.** Digital pre-orders can download from November 12. See the [GTA 6 pre-load guide](/guides/gta-6-pre-load/).
2. **Turn your console on early** on launch day so any day-one update downloads before the unlock.
3. **Sign in** to PlayStation Network or Xbox network ahead of time; servers are busiest right at unlock.
4. **Physical copy?** Redeem the code in the box as soon as you get it, so the download is already done.
