## Global Unlock Times: Rolling Midnight vs. Simultaneous Release

For international territories, release schedules diverge based on how Rockstar configures digital storefront rights:

1. **Rolling Regional Midnight (Most Probable for Console Pre-Orders):** Each international region unlocks as its local clock strikes 12:00 a.m. on November 19. Under this structure, players in New Zealand, Australia, and East Asia unlock the game up to 18 hours before North American players.
2. **Global Simultaneous Release:** The game unlocks at the exact same instant worldwide (e.g., 00:00 UTC). Under a simultaneous model, everyone gains access together regardless of local clock time.

## The "New Zealand Region Trick": Can You Play Early on Xbox or PS5?

The prospect of gaining access up to 18 hours early via New Zealand time zones frequently circulates in gaming forums:

- **On Xbox Series X|S:** Xbox allows users to toggle their console location (**Settings > System > Language & location > Location > New Zealand**). For games that deploy a strict rolling local midnight release, this trick has historically allowed early access. However, if Rockstar enforces server-side authentication tied to billing region or a unified global unlock time, changing the console region will have zero effect.
- **On PlayStation 5:** The New Zealand trick **does not work on PS5**. Sony locks digital software licenses to the specific regional PlayStation Store where the purchase was made. Changing console time or location settings will not alter the countdown timer on your PS5 home screen. Creating a secondary New Zealand PSN account requires purchasing New Zealand PSN currency cards, paying higher retail exchange rates, and forfeiting pre-order bonuses on your primary account. We strongly advise against this.

## Midnight Launch Issues: What to Do If the Padlock Icon Stays Locked

When the countdown timer reaches zero, thousands of consoles contact platform licensing servers simultaneously. If your game remains locked behind a padlock icon:

1. **Wait 60 to 120 Seconds:** Do not immediately delete or reinstall the game. Licensing servers frequently experience brief congestion while processing digital decryption tokens.
2. **Restore Licenses on PS5:** If the lock persists after two minutes, navigate to **Settings > Users and Accounts > Other > Restore Licenses** and select **Restore**. This forces your console to query Sony's network and revalidate digital ownership.
3. **Hard Power-Cycle on Xbox:** Hold the power button on the front of your Xbox console for 10 seconds until it shuts down completely. Unplug the power cable for 30 seconds, reconnect, and boot. This clears cached system tokens.
4. **Network Reconnection:** Disconnect and reconnect your Wi-Fi or Ethernet cable in network settings to establish a fresh connection to authentication clusters.
