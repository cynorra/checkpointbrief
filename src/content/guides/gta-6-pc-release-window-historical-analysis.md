**If GTA 6 follows Rockstar's last three PC ports, the PC version lands between June 2027 and June 2028, with the middle of the range around the end of 2027.** That's a projection from past gaps, not an announcement. Rockstar has not announced a PC version or a date. For what has actually been said, see [what Take-Two has said about GTA 6 on PC](/guides/gta-6-pc-release/); this guide is only the arithmetic.

## Where the early 2027 rumor fits

The [published rumor](/guides/gta-6-pc-release/) says Rockstar is targeting a PC release between January and March 2027. Counting from November 19, 2026, that would mean a gap of roughly 43 to 131 days. That's well under half of the shortest gap Rockstar has ever had (217 days). It isn't impossible, but it would break the pattern by a wide margin, and Rockstar hasn't confirmed it. Treat it as unconfirmed.

## Why the past may not predict this one

The pattern is a reasonable guess, but three things could change it:

- **The sample is small.** Three games over 18 years is not much to build a forecast on. One unusual decision moves the whole range.
- **The platforms are different.** GTA IV and V's PC versions came out as the consoles' generation was ageing. GTA 6 is a current-generation game from day one, so a PC port may be a different amount of work.
- **Rockstar controls the date.** The gap is a business decision as much as an engineering one. Rockstar and Take-Two have not said how they weigh a second wave of sales against an earlier PC release.

## What would change the forecast

Rockstar has typically announced PC versions with a date months ahead, so the first real signal will be a Rockstar or Take-Two announcement, not a leak. Take-Two reports earnings quarterly on its [investor relations site](https://ir.take2games.com/), and Rockstar posts news on its [newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire). Until one of those says something, any specific PC date you see is a guess.

## What this means if you play on PC

- **Planning around late 2027 is a safe default**, with mid-2027 as the optimistic case and mid-2028 as the pessimistic one.
- **Ignore "PC download" and "PC requirements" pages.** None exist. See our note on [fake system requirements](/guides/gta-6-pc-release/).
- **A console purchase doesn't carry over.** Rockstar hasn't mentioned cross-buy or cross-save, so don't assume either.

When Rockstar announces anything about PC, we'll update this guide and the [GTA 6 hub](/gta-6/).
