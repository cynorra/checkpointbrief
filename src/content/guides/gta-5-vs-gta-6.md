**GTA 6 is not a continuation of GTA V's story. It is a new game with new leads, a new setting and new hardware requirements.** GTA V (2013) follows Michael, Franklin and Trevor in Los Santos. GTA 6 (November 19, 2026) follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos in the State of Leonida, runs only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, needs up to 212 GB of storage, and costs $79.99 in its Standard Edition, $20 more than GTA V's launch price. This guide compares GTA 5 and GTA 6 on everything that can be checked against a source, and says plainly where a comparison can't be made yet.

*Last checked November 22, 2026. Figures marked "reported" come from press coverage, not from Rockstar.*

## Is GTA 6 bigger than GTA 5?

Reportedly yes, but nobody outside Rockstar can give you a number. Press coverage of a Rockstar preview said the GTA 6 map is about twice the size of GTA V's, and that Vice City alone is about twice the size of Los Santos. Rockstar has not published a size in square miles or kilometers, so any exact figure you see online is a fan estimate built from trailer footage.

The two games also measure "map" differently: GTA V is one city and a large rural county, while GTA 6 is a whole fictional state with six named regions (Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga). Our [map guide](/guides/gta-6-map-leonida/) lists each region and explains why you should treat size claims carefully.

## GTA 5 vs GTA 6 release dates and platforms

GTA V launched on PS3 and Xbox 360, reached PS4 and Xbox One in November 2014, came to PC in April 2015, and arrived on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S in March 2022. That is three console generations and one PC release across more than eight years. The PC version came out 574 days after the first console release.

GTA 6 starts on the newest consoles only. There is no PS4 or Xbox One version ([why](/guides/gta-6-ps4-xbox-one/)), no Nintendo Switch 2 version has been announced, and Rockstar has not announced a PC release. If GTA V's pattern repeats, a PC version would come well after launch, but that is an inference from history, not a plan. We work through the day counts in our [GTA 6 PC release window analysis](/guides/gta-6-pc-release-window-historical-analysis/) and summarize what has been said in the [PC release guide](/guides/gta-6-pc-release/).

## GTA 5 vs GTA 6 price

Console GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 both launched at $59.99. GTA 6 Standard costs $79.99, which is $20 higher. The Ultimate Edition at $99.99 adds in-game vehicles, weapons, outfits and extra activities. It does not add early access or extra story. Some players have criticized putting cosmetic content behind the higher tier. Details, including what each edition contains, are in our [price and editions guide](/guides/gta-6-price-and-editions/).

## GTA 5 vs GTA 6 file size and storage

Rockstar says the GTA 6 pre-load needs up to 212 GB of free space. GTA V on PS5 takes about 87 GB, so GTA 6 is well over twice as large. On a base PS5 with about 667 GB usable, 212 GB is roughly a third of the drive; on an Xbox Series S with about 364 GB usable, it is more than half. If you are short on space, see our [PS5 SSD upgrade guide](/guides/ps5-ssd-upgrade/) and [Xbox Series storage guide](/guides/xbox-series-storage/), and the [pre-load guide](/guides/gta-6-pre-load/) for download times by internet speed.

## GTA 5 vs GTA 6 characters and story

GTA V told three connected stories: a retired criminal, a young repo man and an unstable partner, all drawn into the same heists. GTA 6 centers on two leads, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, set in the Leonida Keys and Vice City. Rockstar has not described how play will move between the two, so we do not describe it here. What has been shown is covered in our [character guide](/guides/gta-6-jason-and-lucia/), and what remains unknown is in [what we don't know about GTA 6](/guides/gta-6-what-we-dont-know/).

## GTA Online vs GTA 6 multiplayer

GTA V's multiplayer, GTA Online, arrived in October 2013 and is still running. Rockstar has not announced a GTA 6 multiplayer mode for launch, and our [GTA 6 Online guide](/guides/gta-6-online/) tracks what has and hasn't been said. If you mainly play GTA Online, GTA 6's release does not remove it.

## How well did each game sell?

GTA V is, according to its Wikipedia entry, the third-best-selling video game of all time, with almost 235 million copies shipped. Its Wikipedia entry also reports about $800 million in sales on the first day and $1 billion in the first three days. GTA 6 only launched on November 19, 2026, so any sales comparison is too early to make.

## Do you need to play GTA 5 before GTA 6?

There is no sign you do. GTA 6 has new lead characters and a different state, and Rockstar has not said earlier games are required. The Vice City name connects it to the series' history ([our timeline of every mainline game](/guides/gta-series-timeline/) shows how), but that is background, not a prerequisite. This is our reading of what Rockstar has shown, not a Rockstar statement.

## Is GTA 5 still worth playing now that GTA 6 is out?

It depends on your hardware and what you want. GTA V runs on more platforms (PS4, Xbox One, PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC), is far smaller to install, and includes a live online mode. GTA 6 is a newer single-player game that needs a current-generation console and costs more. They are different enough that owning one is not a reason to skip the other.

## GTA 5 vs GTA 6: common questions

**Is GTA 6 a sequel to GTA 5?** It follows GTA V in the series, 13 years later, but the characters, location and story are new.

**Is GTA 6 on PS4?** No. GTA 6 is only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

**Is GTA 6 on PC?** Not announced. GTA V reached PC about 19 months after its first release.

**How much does GTA 6 cost compared with GTA 5?** $79.99 Standard and $99.99 Ultimate, against $59.99 for GTA V at console launch.

**How big is GTA 6 compared with GTA 5?** Up to 212 GB against about 87 GB on PS5.

**Is the GTA 6 map bigger?** Reportedly about twice GTA V's, but Rockstar has not published a size.
