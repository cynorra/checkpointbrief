**GTA 6 is a new game on new hardware, not a sequel to GTA V's story.** It has different leads (Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos instead of Michael, Franklin and Trevor), a different setting (the State of Leonida and Vice City instead of Los Santos), it is only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it costs $20 more at launch. Below is every comparison we can back with a source, and a clear list of the ones nobody can make yet.

## What changes for you as a player

**Hardware.** GTA V reached PS5 and Xbox Series X|S more than eight years after launch, as a re-release. GTA 6 starts there and skips the previous generation entirely. If you only own a PS4 or Xbox One, GTA 6 is not an option; see [why there is no PS4 or Xbox One version](/guides/gta-6-ps4-xbox-one/).

**Price.** Console GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 both launched at $59.99. GTA 6 Standard is $79.99, and the Ultimate Edition at $99.99 adds cosmetic and in-game content rather than early access or extra story.

**Storage.** Rockstar says GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB, well over twice GTA V's roughly 87 GB on PS5. On a base PS5, with about 667 GB usable, that is roughly a third of the drive, so see our [PS5 SSD upgrade guide](/guides/ps5-ssd-upgrade/) and [Xbox Series storage guide](/guides/xbox-series-storage/) before you start.

**Story and characters.** GTA V followed three criminals whose stories converged on a series of heists. GTA 6 follows a pair. Our [character guide](/guides/gta-6-jason-and-lucia/) covers what Rockstar has shown. Because the leads and the state are new, there is no sign you need to have played GTA V first, though Rockstar has not published a statement either way.

**Multiplayer.** GTA V's online mode, GTA Online, is still running. Rockstar has not announced a GTA 6 multiplayer mode for launch, and the details are in our [GTA 6 Online guide](/guides/gta-6-online/). If you mainly play GTA Online, nothing about GTA 6's release takes it away.

## The map: bigger, but not by a number anyone can prove

Press coverage of a Rockstar preview says the GTA 6 map is about twice the size of GTA V's, and Vice City about twice Los Santos. That is reported, not published by Rockstar, and Rockstar has given no official size in square miles or kilometers. Any exact figure you see online is a fan estimate. The full breakdown, with sources, is in our [map guide](/guides/gta-6-map-leonida/).

## What we can't compare yet

- **Frame rate and resolution.** Rockstar's own documentation is the only source we would trust here, and we have not been able to confirm figures, so we are not printing any.
- **Driving, shooting and AI behavior.** These are gameplay impressions, not facts, and differ by who plays.
- **Size of the story.** No official length has been given for either game that would make a fair comparison.

We will add rows to the table as Rockstar publishes them.

## Which one should you play?

If you want a finished, huge online game that runs on almost any hardware, GTA V is still the one. If you have a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S and want Rockstar's newest single-player game, that is GTA 6. They are different enough that owning one is not a reason to skip the other.
