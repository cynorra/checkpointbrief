**The physical edition of GTA 6 does not include a disc. The box contains a code you redeem for the digital download.** Rockstar confirmed this in its pre-order details: "Physical copies of GTAVI will contain a code that can be redeemed for the digital download of the game. A disc will not be included in the box" ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/06/gta-6-physical-edition-doesnt-come-with-discs-its-a-code-in-the-box)). The boxes go on sale November 12, 2026, the same day pre-loading starts.

## What's in the box

- A printed case and cover art
- A download code for the Standard Edition on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S
- No disc

Only the Standard Edition ($79.99) has a physical version. The Ultimate Edition is digital only.

Once redeemed, a code-in-box copy behaves exactly like a digital purchase. It sits in your PlayStation or Xbox library and downloads like any other digital game.

## Why buy the box then?

There are still a few good reasons:

- **Price.** Retailers sometimes discount the physical copy, while console store prices rarely drop before launch.
- **Gifting.** A box is easier to wrap and give than a digital code.
- **Payment.** You can pay in a store, use store credit or a retailer gift card.
- **Collecting.** Some people simply want the case on the shelf.

## Reasons to buy digital instead

- **The GTA+ month.** Rockstar's free month of GTA+ is for digital pre-orders; it isn't mentioned for physical copies. See [GTA 6 pre-order bonuses](/guides/gta-6-pre-order-bonuses/).
- **Pre-load timing.** A digital pre-order starts downloading on November 12 automatically. With a box, you need to have it in hand and redeem it first.
- **Ultimate Edition.** It's only sold digitally.

## Will there be a disc version later?

Rockstar hasn't announced one. Some reports have speculated about a disc release after launch, but nothing official exists. Plan around what's confirmed: a code in a box.

## Make room either way

Because every copy downloads the full game, you need the storage space whichever version you buy.
