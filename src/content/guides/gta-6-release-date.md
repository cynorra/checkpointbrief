**Grand Theft Auto VI releases on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.** That is the date on [Rockstar's official GTA VI page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) and in every store listing. It unlocks at **midnight local time in every region**, according to [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o/grand-theft-auto-vi-regional-pre-load-and-release-times-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-series-x-or-s). There is no PC version at launch. Digital pre-loading opened on November 10.

The date has already moved twice, so it's fair to ask whether it will move again. As of mid-October 2026, Rockstar's answer is no: in an interview with Famitsu, Rockstar's Rob Nelson said the game "will definitely be released on time" ([report](https://ixbt.games/en/news/2026/10/14/rockstar-oficialno-prokommentirovala-datu-vyxoda-gta-vi.html)). Pre-orders have been open since June, the launch trailer is out, pre-loading is live and physical copies reach retailers on November 12.

## Why it was delayed twice

When Trailer 1 came out in December 2023, it ended on a simple promise: 2025. That window held for about a year and a half.

- **May 2025:** Rockstar moved the game from fall 2025 to **May 26, 2026**, saying it needed more time to deliver the quality players expect.
- **November 2025:** Take-Two announced a second move, from May 26 to **November 19, 2026**, again framed as extra polish.

Both delays were announced by Rockstar and Take-Two themselves, not leaked. That matters for how much weight to give the current date: the company that moved it twice is the one now saying it is fixed. A third delay this close to launch, with physical stock already being manufactured, would be far more unusual than the first two.

## What time does it unlock?

**Midnight local time on November 19, in every region.** Rockstar Support says so directly: "Grand Theft Auto VI will release midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions for both PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X|S" ([Rockstar Support, updated November 3](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o/grand-theft-auto-vi-regional-pre-load-and-release-times-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-series-x-or-s)).

That means the game unlocks one time zone at a time, starting in New Zealand and finishing in Hawaii about 23 hours later. In the US, that is 12:00 a.m. Thursday on each coast's own clock: 12:00 a.m. Eastern, 1:00 a.m. Eastern for Central, and 3:00 a.m. Eastern for Pacific. In the UK it is 12:00 a.m. Thursday, and in Japan it is 10:00 a.m. Eastern on Wednesday, November 18. Our [unlock time guide](/guides/gta-6-unlock-time/) has the full table for every major time zone.

An earlier version of this page said the hour was unconfirmed and that all US zones might unlock together at midnight Eastern. Rockstar's support article settled that, so we changed it.

Some outlets also report that changing your account region could unlock the game earlier. Rockstar hasn't said anything about that, and on PS5 a region change can lock you out of your own pre-order. We don't recommend it.

## Is there early access?

No. Neither edition includes early access to the game. The Ultimate Edition adds in-game content, not extra days. Pre-ordering digitally lets you download the game ahead of time so you can play the moment it unlocks, and that's the only head start on offer.

## What to do before launch

If the date holds, the sensible order is:

1. **Decide the edition.** See [price and editions](/guides/gta-6-price-and-editions/). Standard owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade later, which Rockstar confirms.
2. **Check storage.** Rockstar says the pre-load needs up to 212 GB ([file size guide](/guides/gta-6-file-size/)).
3. **Pre-load now** if you pre-ordered digitally: it opened November 10, and the [pre-load guide](/guides/gta-6-pre-load/) covers the steps for PS5 and Xbox.
4. **Pick up the bonus:** the Vintage Vice City Pack applies if you buy before November 20 ([pre-order bonuses](/guides/gta-6-pre-order-bonuses/)).
5. **Prepare launch day:** update the console early and sign in ahead of the unlock.

## Questions people ask

**Is there a PC version on November 19?** No. Rockstar hasn't announced one. See [GTA 6 on PC](/guides/gta-6-pc-release/).

**Can I play on PS4 or Xbox One?** No. The game is for current-generation consoles only. See [GTA 6 on PS4 and Xbox One](/guides/gta-6-ps4-xbox-one/).

**Is the physical edition a disc?** No. The box contains a download code. See [why there is no disc](/guides/gta-6-physical-edition-no-disc/).

**Does the date apply to every country?** The date is November 19, 2026 everywhere, but the local hour it unlocks varies by region.

## Will the date change again?

Nobody outside Rockstar can promise that, but every signal points to November 19:

- Rockstar repeated the date in the October 15 launch trailer.
- Rockstar's Rob Nelson told Famitsu in October that it will "definitely" be on time.
- Pre-loading has already opened, and physical shipments arrive November 12.
