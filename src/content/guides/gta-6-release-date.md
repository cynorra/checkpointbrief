**Grand Theft Auto VI releases on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.** That is the date on [Rockstar's official GTA VI page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) and in every store listing. There is no PC version at launch. Pre-loading opens a week earlier, on November 12.

The date has already moved twice, so it's fair to ask whether it will move again. As of mid-October 2026, Rockstar's answer is no: in an interview with Famitsu, Rockstar's Rob Nelson said the game "will definitely be released on time" ([report](https://ixbt.games/en/news/2026/10/14/rockstar-oficialno-prokommentirovala-datu-vyxoda-gta-vi.html)). Pre-orders have been open since June, the launch trailer is out and physical copies ship November 12.

## Why it was delayed twice

When Trailer 1 came out in December 2023, it ended on a simple promise: 2025. That window held for about a year and a half.

- **May 2025:** Rockstar moved the game from fall 2025 to **May 26, 2026**, saying it needed more time to deliver the quality players expect.
- **November 2025:** Take-Two announced a second move, from May 26 to **November 19, 2026**, again framed as extra polish.

Both delays were announced by Rockstar and Take-Two themselves, not leaked. That matters for how much weight to give the current date: the company that moved it twice is the one now saying it is fixed. A third delay this close to launch, with physical stock already being manufactured, would be far more unusual than the first two.

## What time does it unlock?

Rockstar has announced a date, not a time. Most big console releases unlock at midnight local time in each region, and the PlayStation Store listing points the same way, but Rockstar hasn't published a schedule yet. Don't plan around a specific hour until it does.

## Is there early access?

No. Neither edition includes early access to the game. The Ultimate Edition adds in-game content, not extra days. Pre-ordering digitally lets you download the game ahead of time so you can play the moment it unlocks, and that's the only head start on offer.

## Will the date change again?

Nobody outside Rockstar can promise that, but every signal points to November 19:

- Rockstar repeated the date in the October 15 launch trailer.
- Rockstar's Rob Nelson told Famitsu in October that it will "definitely" be on time.
- Retail pre-orders, the November 12 pre-load and physical shipments are all scheduled around it.
