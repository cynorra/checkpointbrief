## The big one: GTA 6

GTA 6 launches November 19 at $79.99 for the Standard Edition. Digital pre-orders can download it from November 12, and physical copies, which hold a download code instead of a disc, go on sale the same day. Everything confirmed, with sources, is in the [GTA 6 release date guide](/guides/gta-6-release-date/).

## Why November is so quiet otherwise

Publishers plan around big launches. A new Grand Theft Auto is the kind of release that takes players' money and time for weeks, so most companies with a major new game either moved into October (Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 and Phantom Blade Zero both land in late October) or pushed to December and 2027. What's left in November is mostly:

- **Switch 2 editions** of existing games: Stellar Blade, Metaphor: ReFantazio, Pikmin 4 and a new release of Ocarina of Time.
- **Smaller new games** with their own audience, like Edge of Memories and Sonic Pico Park.

If you're not playing GTA 6 at launch, November is a good month to catch up on October's big releases or one of these ports.

## Switch 2 owners

Nintendo's console gets the most November releases by count. Ocarina of Time on November 5 and the Pikmin 4 Switch 2 Edition on November 12 are the two first-party highlights. Use the Switch 2 filter on the [release calendar](/release-dates/) to see only those.

## Dates move

Release dates change, sometimes in the last few weeks. We recheck the calendar regularly and show the check date on the calendar page. Before you pre-order anything, confirm the date on the publisher's store listing.
