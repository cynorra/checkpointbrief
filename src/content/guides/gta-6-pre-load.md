**GTA 6 pre-loading opens on November 12, 2026, for digital pre-orders on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.** That's one week before the game unlocks on November 19. Pre-loading lets you download the game in advance so it's installed and waiting, and you can start playing as soon as it unlocks. The date is in [Rockstar's pre-order announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260624539426/en/Rockstar-Games-Announces-Pre-Orders-for-Grand-Theft-Auto-VI).

The physical edition has no disc, which is why Rockstar released it the same day pre-loading opens: the box contains a code you redeem for the digital download.

## Pre-loading on PS5

1. Make sure the pre-order is on the account you play on.
2. Turn on **Auto-Download** under **Settings → Saved Data and Game/App Settings → Automatic Updates**, and turn on **Stay Connected to the Internet** under **Settings → System → Power Saving → Features Available in Rest Mode**.
3. On or after November 12, the game should start downloading by itself in rest mode. If not, find GTA VI in your **Game Library** and select **Download**.
4. When it's done, the game tile shows a countdown or "available on" date until it unlocks.

## Pre-loading on Xbox Series X|S

1. Make sure the console's power mode is set to **Sleep** (previously "Instant-on") and updates are set to install automatically.
2. Once pre-loading opens, the download normally starts by itself. Otherwise go to **My games & apps → Full library → Owned games**, find GTA VI and select **Install**.
3. Check **My games & apps → Manage queue** to watch progress.

## Check your free space first

Rockstar hasn't published the download size. Expect a very large install, well over 100 GB by most estimates, and leave headroom for a day-one patch. A base PS5 has about 667 GB of usable space and an Xbox Series S about 364 GB, so clear room before November 12, not on the day.

## Does pre-loading mean early access?

No. You can download the game but not play it until it unlocks on November 19. No edition includes early access. What you avoid is the queue: millions of people downloading on launch day.

## The day-one patch

Large games almost always get an update at or just before launch. Even if your pre-load finished on November 12, turn on your console a little early on launch day and let it update, so you're not waiting when it unlocks.
