**GTA 6 pre-loading is open: it began on November 10, 2026, for digital pre-orders on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.** On PS5 it started at midnight local time in each time zone; on Xbox Series X|S it started worldwide at 12 a.m. GMT. Both are in [Rockstar Support's pre-load FAQ](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/7nPqOA2uCbpEQHXx7KVrYd/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-faq). Pre-loading lets you download the game in advance so it's installed and waiting, and you can start playing as soon as it unlocks at midnight local time on November 19.

Earlier coverage, including an earlier version of this guide, gave November 12 as the pre-load date. Rockstar's support article, updated November 3, puts digital pre-load at November 10. November 12 is now the date physical copies reach retailers.

## Pre-loading on PS5

1. Make sure the pre-order is on the account you play on.
2. Turn on **Auto-Download** under **Settings → Saved Data and Game/App Settings → Automatic Updates**, and turn on **Stay Connected to the Internet** under **Settings → System → Power Saving → Features Available in Rest Mode**.
3. The game should start downloading by itself in Rest Mode. Rockstar says auto-downloads must be on and the console powered on or in Rest Mode. If it hasn't started, find GTA VI in your **Game Library** and select **Download**.
4. When it's done, the game tile shows a countdown or "available on" date until it unlocks.

## Pre-loading on Xbox Series X|S

1. Make sure automatic updates are on and the console is powered on or in **Sleep** mode, as Rockstar requires for automatic pre-load.
2. The download normally starts by itself. Otherwise go to **My games & apps → Full library → Owned games**, find GTA VI and select **Install**.
3. Check **My games & apps → Manage queue** to watch progress.

If your connection is under 100 Mbps and you haven't started yet, start now and let it run overnight. The point of pre-loading is that the slow part is finished before launch.

If your internet provider has a monthly data cap, check it first. 212 GB is a large share of many caps, and a patch or a second console on the same account can push you over.

## Pre-load problems and fixes

- **Download won't start:** confirm you're signed in to the account that holds the pre-order, then check the store's download queue. On PS5, make sure the game is listed under your Game Library, not only in the store.
- **Download is slow:** pause and resume it, restart the console, or switch from Wi-Fi to a wired connection. Pausing other downloads on the same network helps more than most settings changes.
- **"Not enough space" warning:** the console needs room for the download plus temporary files while it installs, so having exactly 212 GB free may not be enough. Aim for a comfortable margin.
- **Download finished but the game won't open:** that's expected until the unlock time. The tile will say the game isn't available yet.

## Does pre-loading mean early access?

No. You can download the game but not play it until it unlocks at midnight local time on November 19 (see [the unlock time for your time zone](/guides/gta-6-unlock-time/)). No edition includes early access. What you avoid is the queue: millions of people downloading on launch day.

## The day-one patch

Large games almost always get an update at or just before launch. Even if your pre-load is already finished, turn on your console a little early on launch day and let it update, so you're not waiting when it unlocks.
