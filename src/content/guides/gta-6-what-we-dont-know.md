## Why some numbers keep changing

Many "facts" about GTA 6 come from a chain of reports: a developer says something in a preview, a newspaper reports it, and a dozen sites repeat it, each rounding or reframing it a little. The map is a good example. The "three times RDR2" figure traces back to a report on a Rockstar preview, and different sites describe it as a confirmed fact, an estimate or a rumor. We label each claim by where it started, not by how many sites repeat it.

## Rumors we're not repeating

- **Voice actor names.** Rockstar hasn't announced the cast, and the names that circulate don't even agree with each other for the same character.
- **Exact playtime hours.** Figures from 45 to 500 hours float around. None comes from Rockstar.
- **Leaked builds and downloads.** Never download a supposed GTA 6 file. They are scams or malware.
- **"Insider" weapon and mission details.** Without a source we can link to, these stay out.

## What changes this list on launch day

Most of these questions will have answers within hours of the unlock. File size and the unlock time should appear in the store listings, frame rates and modes in the settings menu, and the online plans in whatever Rockstar chooses to say. Some, such as a PC version, may stay unanswered for months. We'll update the guides linked above as each one moves, and the [GTA 6 hub](/gta-6/) tracks the confirmed items.

For contrast, these are firm, from Rockstar's own announcements ([official page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI), [pre-order release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260624539426/en/Rockstar-Games-Announces-Pre-Orders-for-Grand-Theft-Auto-VI)):

- Rated **M for Mature 17+** by the ESRB.

## How to read GTA 6 news in launch week

The closer a release gets, the more confident-sounding claims appear. A quick test:

1. **Who said it?** Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony or Microsoft can confirm. An "insider" can't.
2. **Is there a link?** A real announcement has a source: a press release, a store listing or an official post.
3. **Is it a number with no source?** File sizes, map sizes and fps figures without a link are guesses.
