## What is confirmed

For contrast, these are firm, from Rockstar's own announcements ([official page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI), [pre-order release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260624539426/en/Rockstar-Games-Announces-Pre-Orders-for-Grand-Theft-Auto-VI)):

- Release on **November 19, 2026**, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- **$79.99** Standard and **$99.99** Ultimate.
- Pre-load and physical copies on **November 12**.
- Vintage Vice City Pack for purchases before November 20; a free GTA+ month for digital pre-orders.
- Set in **Leonida**, with **Vice City**, starring **Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos**.
- Rated **M for Mature 17+** by the ESRB.

## How to read GTA 6 news in launch week

The closer a release gets, the more confident-sounding claims appear. A quick test:

1. **Who said it?** Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony or Microsoft can confirm. An "insider" can't.
2. **Is there a link?** A real announcement has a source: a press release, a store listing or an official post.
3. **Is it a number with no source?** File sizes, map sizes and fps figures without a link are guesses.
