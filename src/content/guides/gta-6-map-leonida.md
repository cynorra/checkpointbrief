**GTA 6 takes place in Leonida, a fictional US state modeled on Florida. Its main city is Vice City, Rockstar's take on Miami, and the rest of the state includes swamps, islands, beaches, farmland and a national park.** Rockstar has introduced these places through its trailers and the [official GTA VI site](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI); there's no official map image yet, and we don't use leaked ones.

The inspirations in the third column are how the places are widely described, including on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI); Rockstar frames them as fictional. Within Vice City, Wikipedia also lists Stockyard, a neighborhood based on Miami's Wynwood arts district.

## Vice City

The heart of the game. Trailers show a modern Miami-style city: beachfront, high-rises, clubs and highways. It's the same city name as 2002's GTA: Vice City, but rebuilt for the present day. Rockstar's site describes it as the place where both protagonists' story centers.

## Leonida Keys

A chain of islands connected by long bridges, like the real Florida Keys. Jason Duval's background is tied to the Keys, where he worked before the events of the game.

## Grassrivers

Leonida's version of the Everglades: wetlands, airboats and wildlife. Trailers lean into this as one of the most distinctive parts of the map, because nothing like it has appeared in a GTA game before.

## Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga

These round out the state outside the city. Port Gellhorn is a run-down coastal town; Ambrosia is an inland industrial and agricultural area; Mount Kalaga National Park offers hills, forests and trails. Together they give the map a range beyond the beach-and-neon city.

## How big is the map?

Rockstar hasn't released an official map. Wikipedia quotes Rockstar describing it as "three times larger than the playable area of Red Dead Redemption 2." Treat any exact square-mile figure you see as an estimate. Fan maps are built from trailer footage and, sometimes, leaked material, and they're often wrong in the details.

## Will more places be revealed?

Very likely. Pre-launch marketing never shows everything, and Rockstar games usually hold back parts of the map for players to discover. We'll add new named locations to the [GTA 6 hub](/gta-6/) as Rockstar reveals them.
