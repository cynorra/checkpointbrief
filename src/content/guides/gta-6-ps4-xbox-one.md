**No. GTA 6 is only for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no PS4 or Xbox One version, and none has ever been announced.** Rockstar lists only current-generation consoles on the [official GTA VI page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) and in its pre-order announcement.

## Why last-generation consoles were left out

The PS4 and Xbox One launched in 2013. GTA 6 was built for hardware that is much faster in the ways open-world games depend on most:

- **Fast SSD storage.** PS5 and Xbox Series consoles stream the world from solid-state drives many times faster than the hard drives in PS4 and Xbox One. A dense, detailed city needs that.
- **Processors.** The current consoles' CPUs are several times more capable, which matters for crowds, traffic and simulation.
- **Memory.** More, faster memory for higher-detail assets.

Supporting both generations would mean designing the game around the slowest hardware. Rockstar didn't do that.

## Could it change?

It's very unlikely. Rockstar has released no last-generation version in any of its marketing, store listings or press materials. Late in a console generation, publishers move away from older hardware, not back to it.

## Will a PS4 game disc or code work on PS5?

There's nothing to transfer: GTA 6 doesn't exist on PS4. If you see a "PS4 version" for sale, it's a mistake or a scam.

## Bundles

Console bundles with big games are common around major launches. If one is announced for GTA 6, it will be listed by Sony or Microsoft and major retailers. Compare it against buying the console and game separately; bundles aren't always cheaper.
