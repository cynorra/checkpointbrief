## Trailer 1: December 2023

Rockstar had planned to release the first trailer in early December 2023. A low-quality copy leaked online on December 4, so Rockstar released the real thing early, on December 5. It confirmed the name Grand Theft Auto VI, the return to Vice City, introduced Lucia and ended with "2025". It broke YouTube viewing records for a non-music video within a day.

## Trailer 2: May 6, 2025

Trailer 2 arrived just days after the first delay to May 2026 was announced. It centered on Jason and Lucia as a couple, introduced the supporting cast and came with 70 screenshots and character and location profiles on Rockstar's site. Rockstar said it was captured on a PS5.

## Extended look: August 27, 2026

The longest look at the game before launch. It premiered on Netflix, a first for a Rockstar game, a few hours before it went up on YouTube, and focused more on how the game plays than the earlier story-driven trailers.

## Launch trailer: October 15, 2026

Released at 2 p.m. London time (9 a.m. Eastern) on October 15, five weeks before release. Its description says it was "captured entirely in-game from a PlayStation 5." It follows Jason and Lucia through a criminal conspiracy across Leonida and features new music from the French electronic duo Justice ([playnews.gg](https://www.playnews.gg/en/trailers/gta-6-trailer-3-is-out-the-launch-trailer-shot-in-game-on-ps5-ahead-of-19-november)).
