**No PC version of GTA 6 has been announced. The game launches November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and Rockstar has given no date or even a window for PC.** Take-Two's CEO has said PC players aren't the core audience for the launch ([God is a Geek](https://godisageek.com/2026/05/gta-6-skips-pc-at-launch-consoles-come-first/)), which is consistent with how Rockstar has released its last several games.

## The early 2027 rumor

A report from GamesBeat on October 13, 2026 says Rockstar is planning a PC release between January and March 2027, citing anonymous sources ([summary](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/23463/gta-6-pc-release-date-q1-2027-rumor-gamesbeat), [Outlook India](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/gta-6-pc-release-reportedly-targets-early-2027)). Rockstar hasn't confirmed it, and Take-Two's CEO has said only that Rockstar's games don't always launch on every platform at the same time ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/take-two-ceo-talks-about-not-announcing-gta-6-on-pc/)). A window that short would be much faster than any of Rockstar's earlier PC ports, so treat it as a rumor until Rockstar says so itself.

On that pattern, a PC version roughly a year or more after launch would be normal for Rockstar. But it's a pattern, not a promise. Nothing official says when.

## Why consoles first?

Rockstar hasn't explained it in detail, but the reasons usually given are practical:

- **Fewer hardware targets.** Consoles have a small number of fixed configurations; PCs have endless combinations.
- **Piracy and cheating.** A later PC release keeps the launch window on platforms that are harder to crack.
- **Sales.** Selling the same game again on PC a year later brings a second wave of sales.

## If you only play on PC

You have three realistic options:

1. **Wait.** If past releases are a guide, a PC version is likely eventually, and it may arrive with improvements.
2. **Buy a console.** Any PS5 or Xbox Series X|S will run it. The Series S is the cheapest way in.
3. **Watch out for scams.** There is no PC download of GTA 6. Sites and videos offering one are scams, often bundled with malware.

A console purchase doesn't carry over to PC. Rockstar hasn't said anything about cross-buy, cross-save or discounts for console owners, so don't assume any of them.

## Ignore fake system requirements

Websites publish "GTA 6 PC requirements" lists, but Rockstar hasn't released any, since it hasn't announced a PC version. These tables are guesses and sometimes link to downloads. Real requirements normally appear on Rockstar's own site and on Steam or the Rockstar launcher, usually weeks before a PC release.
