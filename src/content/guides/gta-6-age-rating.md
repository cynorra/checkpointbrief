**GTA 6 is rated M (Mature 17+) by the ESRB.** Its content descriptors are Blood and Gore, Intense Violence, Mature Humor, Nudity, Strong Language, Strong Sexual Content and Use of Drugs and Alcohol ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-age-ratings-suggest-no-gambling-to-go-with-drugs-and-decapitations-3416139/)). It's the same rating every main Grand Theft Auto game has had, and it means the ESRB considers it suitable for players 17 and older.

For the official wording, look up the game on the [ESRB's site](https://www.esrb.org/), which also lists a short rating summary.

## Is there gambling?

The current ratings don't include a gambling descriptor. That suggests there are no playable casino games in the base game, but it isn't a confirmation, and ratings can't say anything about an online mode that hasn't launched yet.

## How it compares to GTA V

GTA V was also rated M by the ESRB and 18 by PEGI in Europe. GTA 6 lands in the same category. If you've decided GTA V isn't right for someone in your house, GTA 6 won't be different.

On both consoles you can block games above a chosen age rating, so an M-rated game won't launch on a child's profile without a parent's approval.
