**GTA+ is Rockstar's monthly subscription on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It gives you the GTA+ Games Library, a collection of older Rockstar games you can play while subscribed, plus monthly bonuses in GTA Online.** Digital pre-orders of GTA 6 come with one month free, which you can redeem right away ([Rockstar's announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260624539426/en/Rockstar-Games-Announces-Pre-Orders-for-Grand-Theft-Auto-VI)).

## How much does GTA+ cost?

In the US, GTA+ costs **$7.99 a month**. It launched at $5.99 and was raised to $7.99 in April 2024 ([GTA Intel](https://gtaintel.com/news/gta-online-plus-subscription-explained)). Prices vary by region and platform store, so check your own store's price.

The main recurring GTA Online benefit is a monthly in-game cash payment of GTA$500,000, deposited automatically. On top of that, Rockstar rotates extra perks (free vehicles, bonus-pay weeks and discounts) every several weeks. Rockstar's [GTA+ benefits page](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/21l14aEXmnyjFrnsqQ6eXG/gta-membership-benefits) is the authority on what's current, since the list changes.

## The free month with GTA 6

Rockstar says digital pre-orders of GTA 6 include a free month of GTA+ that is "instantly redeemable." In practice that means you can use it now, before GTA 6 launches, to play the library games. Two things to know:

- **It's for digital pre-orders.** The announcement doesn't mention it for physical copies, and one outlet reports Xbox pre-orders don't receive it, so check the listing on your platform.
- **The deadline is reported as before November 20.** Some sources say November 19. Check the store's terms before you buy.
- **The clock starts when you redeem it.** If you'd rather use it later, check whether your store lets you hold the code.

See [GTA 6 pre-order bonuses](/guides/gta-6-pre-order-bonuses/) for the full list.

## Does GTA+ do anything in GTA 6?

Not that Rockstar has announced. GTA 6 launches as a single-player game, and GTA+ benefits so far apply to GTA Online and the Games Library. If a new online mode arrives later, GTA+ could well be extended to it, but that's speculation. See [GTA 6 Online: is there multiplayer at launch?](/guides/gta-6-online/)

## Do you need PlayStation Plus or Game Pass too?

GTA+ is separate from Sony's and Microsoft's subscriptions. Single-player library games don't need either. Playing GTA Online itself requires PlayStation Plus on PS5 or an Xbox subscription that includes online play.

## How to avoid paying after the free month

Subscriptions renew automatically. If you only want the free month:

- **PS5:** Settings → Users and Accounts → Account → Payment and Subscriptions → Subscriptions, then turn off auto-renew for GTA+.
- **Xbox:** sign in at account.microsoft.com → Services & subscriptions, and turn off recurring billing for GTA+.

Turning off auto-renew keeps the benefits until the end of the paid or free period.
