**There have been seven numbered or main Grand Theft Auto games before GTA 6 in nearly 30 years, plus expansions, handheld spin-offs and remasters.** GTA 6, on November 19, 2026, is the first new main entry since GTA V in 2013, the longest gap in the series' history.

## Re-releases

- **Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition** (2021): remastered GTA III, Vice City and San Andreas.
- **GTA V on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S** (March 2022): upgraded version for current consoles.
- **GTA V Enhanced on PC** (March 2025): the current-generation upgrade brought to PC.

## The long wait for GTA 6

Before GTA V, a new main game arrived every few years. The twelve-plus years between GTA V and GTA 6 are partly explained by GTA Online's success, which kept GTA V selling for over a decade, and by Rockstar's work on Red Dead Redemption 2 in between. The history of GTA 6's own announcement, trailers and delays is in the [GTA 6 release date guide](/guides/gta-6-release-date/), and the return to its most famous city is covered in [Vice City then and now](/guides/vice-city-then-and-now/).
