**Spanning nearly three decades, the Grand Theft Auto franchise has released seven numbered mainline titles, four standalone handheld spin-offs, two episodic expansions, and multiple generational remasters.** Grand Theft Auto VI, launching November 19, 2026, marks the first new mainline entry since GTA V debuted in September 2013—representing an unprecedented thirteen-year development gap that reshaped how Rockstar Games and parent company Take-Two Interactive approach open-world production.

## The Three Distinct Universes and Canon Continuity

Rockstar Games explicitly divides the franchise into three distinct narrative universes to maintain creative freedom and explain inconsistencies in geography and character design:

1. **The 2D Universe (1997–1999):** Encompasses *GTA 1*, *London 1969*, and *GTA 2*. Top-down arcade gameplay with loosely connected criminal missions.
2. **The 3D Universe (2001–2006):** Encompasses *GTA III*, *Vice City*, *San Andreas*, *Advance*, *Liberty City Stories*, and *Vice City Stories*. Characters cross paths frequently (e.g., Claude appearing in San Andreas, Donald Love in Vice City), but their stories are self-contained within this era.
3. **The HD Universe (2008–Present):** Beginning with *GTA IV* and continuing through *GTA V* and *Grand Theft Auto VI*. In this continuity, cities were re-engineered with real-world architectural proportions. Characters from the 3D era (such as Tommy Vercetti or CJ) exist only as cultural myths, easter eggs, or brand names, not living characters. [Jason and Lucia's story](/guides/gta-6-jason-and-lucia/) in GTA 6 takes place entirely within this HD continuity.

### Why Did GTA 6 Take 13 Years?
The long interval between mainline entries is attributable to three major structural shifts:
- **Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018):** Rockstar consolidated all global studios into a single unified development pipeline to build RDR2, requiring five years of dedicated studio-wide effort after GTA V.
- **GTA Online's Commercial Longevity:** According to [Take-Two Interactive SEC filings](https://ir.take2games.com/), GTA V has sold over 200 million lifetime copies, continually buoyed by seasonal online updates that sustained unprecedented player engagement without requiring a hasty sequel.
- **Engine Simulation Complexity:** GTA 6 required building new physics, fluid dynamics, and crowd AI routines exclusively tailored for current-generation SSD bandwidth. ## Generational Re-Releases and Remasters

Rather than rushing new entries, Rockstar bridged console generations with updated editions:
- **GTA V Across Three Generations:** Released on PS3/Xbox 360 (2013), PS4/Xbox One (2014), PC (2015), and native PS5/Xbox Series X|S (2022).
- **Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition (2021):** Unreal Engine 4 remaster of III, Vice City, and San Andreas for modern platforms.
