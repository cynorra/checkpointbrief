**GTA 6 is the same game on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S: same release date (November 19, 2026), same price ($79.99 and $99.99), same content.** The differences Rockstar has confirmed are about each console's own features, mainly PlayStation's DualSense controller and 3D audio. Rockstar has not published frame rates or resolutions for any console.

## The PlayStation marketing deal

PlayStation is GTA 6's marketing partner. That's why the launch trailer was "captured entirely in-game from a PlayStation 5" and why Rockstar has said more about the PS5 version. A marketing deal shapes the trailers and ads; it doesn't by itself mean the game runs better on PS5.

## What "PS5 Pro Enhanced" means

The label means the developer has done extra work for the more powerful PS5 Pro, typically higher resolution, better image quality or a steadier frame rate. Rockstar hasn't said what that work is for GTA 6. Treat it as "it will look or run somewhat better on Pro," not as a specific promise like 60 fps.

## 30 or 60 fps?

Unknown. Rockstar hasn't said. A leak claimed there would be performance and quality modes, but not what frame rate performance mode targets. Some technical analysts have doubted that a stable 60 fps is realistic for a game this size, even on Pro. Wait for Rockstar or launch-day testing before buying hardware for it.

## The Xbox Series S question

The Series S is less powerful than the Series X and PS5, and has much less storage. GTA 6 is confirmed for it, so it will run, but expect lower resolution than the Series X. The bigger practical issue is space: a 512 GB Series S has about 364 GB usable. See [GTA 6 file size](/guides/gta-6-file-size/).

## Which should you buy it on?

- **The console your friends play on**, especially if online play arrives later.
- **The console you already own.** The differences aren't worth buying a second console for.
- **If you're buying new:** a standard PS5 or Xbox Series X are both safe choices. The PS5 Pro is the option if you want whatever Rockstar's Pro enhancements turn out to be.
