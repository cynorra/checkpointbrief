**GTA 6 unlocks on November 19, 2026, and pre-loading opens November 12.** Most launch-day problems are things you can fix in advance: not enough space, a download that never started, a console update waiting, or a code still sitting in its box. Work through this list during launch week and you'll be playing while others are still downloading.

## 1. Free up space

Rockstar hasn't published a file size, but plan for a large install plus a patch. The [file size guide](/guides/gta-6-file-size/) shows the real usable space on every console and the fastest ways to clear room. If you're always full, consider [adding an SSD to a PS5](/guides/ps5-ssd-upgrade/) or an [Xbox expansion card](/guides/xbox-series-storage/).

## 2–4. Get the console ready

Install any pending system update now; consoles sometimes refuse to start a game until they're updated. Then make sure the pre-order is on the account you actually play on, and turn on automatic downloads so the pre-load starts by itself. Settings for both consoles are in the [pre-load guide](/guides/gta-6-pre-load/).

## 5–6. Pre-load or redeem

Digital pre-orders can download from November 12. If you bought the physical edition, remember there's [no disc](/guides/gta-6-physical-edition-no-disc/): redeem the code as soon as you have the box, and the download starts like a digital copy. A day or two later, check the download queue to make sure it finished and didn't stall.

## 7. GTA+

Digital pre-orders include a free month of GTA+, redeemable right away. Use it now to play older Rockstar games, or hold off. Either way, note when it renews. See [what is GTA+](/guides/what-is-gta-plus/).

## 8–9. Launch eve

Sign in the night before. Rockstar hasn't published an official unlock time; see [what time GTA 6 unlocks](/guides/gta-6-unlock-time/) for the reported schedule. Turn the console on a little early so any day-one update installs before the game opens.

## 10. TV settings

Switch your TV to its game mode for lower input lag, and run your console's HDR calibration if you haven't. It takes two minutes and makes a visible difference in a bright, sunny game like this one.

## One more thing: spoilers

Story clips and leaks spread fast in launch week. If you want to go in fresh, mute "GTA 6" keywords on social apps for a few days.
