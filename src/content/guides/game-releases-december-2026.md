**December 2026 is busier than November: with GTA 6 out of the way, more than a dozen games have a confirmed date, mostly in the first two weeks.** Highlights include Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV on PC, Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World and the full release of Path of Exile 2. The list is compiled from the [VGC release schedule](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/upcoming-game-release-dates-schedule/) and publisher announcements; check the [release date calendar](/release-dates/) for the latest version.

## December 3 is the big day

Five games land on Thursday, December 3, including Dawn of War IV, the first new entry in the real-time strategy series in years, and the latest Dragon Quest Monsters, which is on nearly every platform including both Switch consoles.

## Switch 2 keeps getting ports

As in November, a large share of December's dates are Switch 2 versions of existing games: Monster Hunter Wilds, Xenoblade Chronicles 3 and The Ascent. If you've just bought a Switch 2, this is a good month to fill out the library.

## Why the second half is empty

Very few publishers release games in the last two weeks of December. Holiday shopping decisions are made by then, and review coverage is thin over the holidays. Expect the calendar to pick up again in January.

If you're still deciding what to play after GTA 6, the [November 2026 release list](/guides/game-releases-november-2026/) covers everything from early November, including Ocarina of Time and Pikmin 4 on Switch 2.
