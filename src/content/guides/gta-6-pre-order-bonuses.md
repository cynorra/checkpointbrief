**There are two GTA 6 pre-order bonuses: the Vintage Vice City Pack, for any pre-order or purchase made before November 20, 2026, and one free month of GTA+, for digital pre-orders only.** Both come from [Rockstar's pre-order announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260624539426/en/Rockstar-Games-Announces-Pre-Orders-for-Grand-Theft-Auto-VI). Neither gives early access to the game.

## The Vintage Vice City Pack

The pack is cosmetic and convenience content with a retro Vice City theme:

- Outfits and hairstyles for both protagonists, Jason and Lucia
- A '55 Vapid Stanier classic sedan, with a garage and weapon locker
- A tropical palm-tree weapon pattern

([contents via PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-pre-order-editions-standard-ultimate/))

**Who gets it:** anyone who pre-orders, or buys the game before November 20, 2026, on either edition, digital or physical. In other words, you don't strictly need to pre-order. Buying in launch week still counts.

## The free month of GTA+

GTA+ is Rockstar's subscription. The press release says digital pre-orders get a month free, and that it's redeemable right away, which means you can use it before GTA 6 is out. The main thing it gives you today is the GTA+ Games Library, a rotating set of older Rockstar games you can play while subscribed, including Grand Theft Auto V.

**Who gets it:** digital pre-orders. Rockstar's announcement doesn't mention the GTA+ month for physical copies, so if that month matters to you, buy digital.

## Is it worth pre-ordering for these?

The bonuses alone aren't a reason to pre-order. The pack is small, and Rockstar often sells or re-releases launch bonuses later. The real advantage of a digital pre-order is **pre-loading**: from November 12, you can download the whole game so it's ready to play the moment it unlocks, instead of waiting on a very large download on launch day.

If you're certain you'll buy GTA 6 at launch, a digital pre-order gets you both bonuses and the pre-load. If you're not sure yet, there's no rush; the pack is still yours if you buy in launch week.

## Can you cancel a pre-order?

Store policies differ and they change, so check the current rules on the store you use. PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store both allow pre-order cancellations under conditions that depend on how close the release is. Retailers set their own rules for physical pre-orders.
