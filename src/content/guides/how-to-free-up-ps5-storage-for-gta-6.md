**To make room for Grand Theft Auto 6 on a PS5, move PS4 games to a USB extended-storage drive, delete or copy large video captures, and confirm your saves are backed up to the cloud.** Rockstar says the pre-load needs up to 212 GB (see our [GTA 6 file size guide](/guides/gta-6-file-size/)), so aim for more free space than you think you need. Pre-loading has been open since November 10, 2026.

## Assessing Current PS5 Storage & Identifying Data Targets

Before initiating any data transfers or deletions, it is critical to understand the current storage allocation on your PlayStation 5. Rockstar says the Grand Theft Auto 6 pre-load needs up to 212 GB of free space, so plan for a very large download. Identifying the largest non-essential data blocks is the first step.

**To check your PS5's current storage:**
1.  Navigate to **Settings** (gear icon on the Home screen).
2.  Select **Storage**.
3.  Here, you will see a breakdown of your Console Storage, including Games and Apps, Media Gallery, Saved Data, and Other.

**Prioritization for Space Reclamation:**
*   **PS4 Games:** These are the primary targets for external storage migration. PS4 titles can be played directly from a compatible USB external hard drive, freeing up the faster internal SSD for PS5-native games.
*   **PS5 Games:** While PS5 games cannot be *played* from an external USB drive, they can be *stored* there and then transferred back to the internal SSD when needed. This is a slower process than simply deleting and re-downloading for smaller titles, but useful for large, infrequently played PS5 games.
*   **Media Gallery (Game Captures):** Video clips, especially 4K recordings, consume substantial space. These can be deleted or transferred to a USB drive for archival.
*   **Saved Data:** Game save files are typically very small (MBs, not GBs) and should only be deleted if you are certain you will never play the game again or if a cloud backup is confirmed.

## Migrating PS4 Games to External USB Storage

Moving PS4 games to an external USB drive is the most efficient method for freeing up internal PS5 SSD space without losing access to your game library. A compatible external drive must be USB 3.0 or later, with a minimum capacity of 250GB and a maximum of 8TB.

**Step-by-Step PS4 Game Transfer:**
1.  **Connect USB Drive:** Plug a compatible USB external hard drive into an available USB-A port on your PS5.
2.  **Format for PS5:** If it's a new drive or not formatted for PS5, the system will prompt you to format it as "Extended Storage."
    *   Go to **Settings > Storage > Extended Storage**.
    *   Select **Format as Extended Storage**. Confirm the action. *Note: This will erase all data on the drive.*
3.  **Move Games:**
    *   From the **Storage** menu, select **Console Storage**.
    *   Choose **Games and Apps**.
    *   Select **Items You Can Move**.
    *   Check the boxes next to the PS4 games you wish to move.
    *   Select **Move** on the bottom right.
    *   Confirm the transfer to **Extended Storage**.

This process will transfer the selected PS4 games from your internal SSD to the external drive. You can then launch and play these games directly from the external drive. For more details on storage options, including internal SSD upgrades, refer to our guide on [How to Add Storage to a PS5: M.2 SSD Requirements and Steps](/guides/ps5-ssd-upgrade/).

## Optimizing Internal PS5 Storage: Captures and Save Data

While PS4 games offer the largest gains, managing your Media Gallery and ensuring save data integrity are crucial steps in a comprehensive storage clear-out.

### Managing Game Captures (Media Gallery)

Video clips, especially those recorded at 4K resolution, can quickly accumulate gigabytes of data. Screenshots are smaller but can still add up.

**To delete or transfer game captures:**
1.  Navigate to **Settings > Storage > Console Storage**.
2.  Select **Media Gallery**.
3.  Here, you can view your screenshots and video clips.
4.  **To Delete:** Select the items you wish to remove, press the **Options button** on your controller, and choose **Delete**.
5.  **To Transfer to USB:** Insert a separate USB drive (not the one formatted for Extended Storage, as it's for games only) into a PS5 USB-A port.
    *   From the Media Gallery, select the items you want to transfer.
    *   Press the **Options button** and choose **Copy to USB Drive**.
    *   Select the destination USB drive and confirm.

### Verifying and Backing Up Save Data

Game save files are small but critical. Deleting them without a backup means losing all progress. PlayStation Plus subscribers benefit from automatic cloud backups.

**To check cloud save status and manually back up:**
1.  Navigate to **Settings > Saved Data and Game/App Settings**.
2.  Select **Saved Data (PS5)** or **Saved Data (PS4)**.
3.  Choose **Console Storage**.
4.  **For PS Plus Subscribers (Cloud Sync):**
    *   Select **Sync Saved Data**.
    *   Ensure **Auto-Sync Saved Data** is enabled for automatic uploads.
    *   You can also manually **Upload to Cloud Storage** for specific games.
5.  **For Non-PS Plus Subscribers (USB Backup):**
    *   Insert a USB drive (again, not the Extended Storage drive) into a PS5 USB-A port.
    *   From the **Console Storage** tab, select the game saves you want to back up.
    *   Select **Copy to USB Drive**.

## Preparing for GTA 6 Pre-Load and Installation

With internal storage cleared, the final steps involve configuring your PS5 for a seamless Grand Theft Auto 6 pre-load and installation. Pre-loading for GTA 6 opened on November 10, 2026, and the game unlocks at midnight local time on November 19, 2026.

**Key Preparations:**
*   **Confirm Space:** Double-check your available Console Storage via **Settings > Storage**. Leave a comfortable margin above Rockstar's 212 GB figure.
*   **Enable Automatic Downloads:** This ensures the pre-load initiates automatically once available.
    *   Go to **Settings > System > Power Saving > Features Available in Rest Mode**.
    *   Ensure **Stay Connected to the Internet** is enabled.
    *   Go to **Settings > Saved Data and Game/App Settings > Automatic Updates**.
    *   Enable **Auto-Download** and **Auto-Install in Rest Mode**.
*   **Rest Mode Configuration:** For optimal pre-load efficiency, especially for large titles like GTA 6, ensure your PS5 is set to download updates while in Rest Mode. This allows the console to download the game files in the background without requiring active use. More details on this can be found in our [PS5 Pre-Load Explained: 7-Day Early Downloads, Auto-Install, and Storage](/guides/what-does-pre-load-mean-on-ps5/) guide.
*   **Network Stability:** A stable and fast internet connection is crucial for a download this large. Consider a wired Ethernet connection over Wi-Fi if possible, or ensure your Wi-Fi signal is strong.
