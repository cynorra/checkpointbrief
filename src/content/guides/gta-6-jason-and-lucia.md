**GTA 6 has two protagonists, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, a couple pulled into crime in the state of Leonida.** Lucia is the first female protagonist the series makes you play as, rather than offering as an optional pick. Rockstar introduced both, plus several supporting characters, in Trailer 2 and on the [official GTA VI site](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI). Here's what Rockstar has shared, without spoilers beyond the marketing.

## Lucia Caminos

Rockstar presents Lucia as someone who has had to fight since childhood. When the game begins, she's coming out of Leonida Penitentiary, where she ended up after trying to look after her family. She wants a better life and is willing to take big risks to get it.

## Jason Duval

Jason grew up around grifters and drifters. After a short stint in the Army, he ended up working for drug runners in the Leonida Keys, and he's looking for a way out of that life. Meeting Lucia changes his plans.

## The two-hander idea

GTA V had three protagonists you could switch between. GTA 6's marketing focuses on two people with a shared story, which is closer to a crime road movie. Rockstar hasn't explained exactly how switching between them works in play.

The change matters because a story built around one couple allows a different tone from GTA V's triangle of unrelated men. Rockstar hasn't explained how much you will control each character, so don't assume you can freely swap the way you could in GTA V.

## Who voices them?

Rockstar hasn't officially confirmed the voice and motion-capture cast. Names circulating online are unconfirmed, and different sites name different people for the same character, so we don't list them. When Rockstar publishes credits, we'll add them.

## What we don't know

- How the story ends, of course, and how many endings there are.
- How much you can switch between Jason and Lucia.
- Whether characters from earlier games appear.
