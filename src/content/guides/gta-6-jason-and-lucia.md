**Grand Theft Auto VI stars dual protagonists Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, marking the franchise's first dedicated two-hander narrative and the first female lead of the series' modern HD universe.** Set against the neon-lit backdrop of Vice City and the sprawling State of Leonida, their storyline moves away from the three-way criminal triangle of Grand Theft Auto V toward a high-stakes, modern Bonnie-and-Clyde partnership.

### Lucia Caminos: Breaking New Ground in the HD Era
Lucia represents a landmark milestone for the Grand Theft Auto franchise. While the 1997 top-down original allowed players to select pixelated female skins (such as Divine or Mikki), Lucia is the first female protagonist with a fully written, performance-captured, and non-optional starring role in the franchise's 3D and HD history. 

Her opening dialogue in Trailer 1—"The only way we're gonna get through this is by sticking together, being a team"—establishes mutual dependency as the core thematic pillar of the campaign.

### Jason Duval: The Military Veteran Turned Smuggler
Jason's military background provides combat and tactical proficiency, contrasting with Lucia’s pragmatic street navigation. Operating out of the sun-bleached trailer parks and boat docks of the Leonida Keys, his connection to local smuggling rings provides the gateway into Vice City's high-stakes underground economy.

## Performance Capture and Voice Cast Facts

Rockstar utilizes state-of-the-art optical performance capture, simultaneously recording vocal dialogue, facial expressions, and bodily movements on high-density soundstages. 

- **Official Stance on Cast Names:** Rockstar Games has intentionally withheld official actor credits to protect cast members from early media harassment and NDA breaches prior to launch.
- **Online Casting Rumors:** Various internet forums have circulated unconfirmed names for Jason and Lucia. Rockstar has not validated any of these claims; cast credits will be officially revealed alongside the final credits scroll upon launch.
- **No Cameos from 3D Era Characters:** Because GTA 6 is set in the separate HD Universe, characters from the 2002 PS2 game (such as Tommy Vercetti) do not appear in the narrative ([see our Vice City Then and Now retrospective](/guides/vice-city-then-and-now/)).

## Themes: Romance, Social Media Satire, and Survival

Beyond heists, GTA 6 explores how criminal enterprises intersect with hyper-connected modern culture:
1. **The Modern Surveillance State:** Smartphone cameras, viral livestream feeds, and police bodycam footage play an active role in how crimes are reported and tracked across Vice City.
2. **Trust as Currency:** The narrative centers on vulnerability—can two criminals genuinely trust one another when law enforcement and rival syndicates apply maximum pressure?
3. **Leonida's Extremes:** From the opulent billionaire enclaves of Vice City Beach to the lawless alligator-filled swamps of the Grassrivers, Jason and Lucia must adapt to wildly contrasting environments.
