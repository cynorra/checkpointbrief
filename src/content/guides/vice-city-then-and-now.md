**GTA 6 returns to Vice City, Rockstar's fictional Miami, for the first time in a new main game since 2002.** The original GTA: Vice City was set in 1986 and became one of the most loved games in the series. GTA 6 rebuilds the city for the present day and makes it the center of a whole state, Leonida.

Vice City was one of three cities in the very first Grand Theft Auto in 1997, alongside Liberty City and San Andreas, but that version was a small top-down map with little in common with the later games.

## Vice City (2002): the 1980s classic

Rockstar's 2002 game leaned completely into the decade: neon, pastel suits, synth-pop radio and crime-movie influences. It brought motorcycles and flyable helicopters to the 3D games and was praised especially for its licensed 1980s soundtrack. It was a single city of two main islands.

## Vice City Stories (2006)

A prequel originally made for the PlayStation Portable, set two years earlier, in 1984. It reused the same map with changes for the earlier year.

## GTA 6: Vice City today

GTA 6's Vice City is modern: social media, nightlife, condo towers and highway traffic, all shown in the trailers. It's much denser than the 2002 map, and it isn't the whole game. It sits within Leonida, with the Keys, the Grassrivers wetlands and inland towns around it. See [every Leonida location Rockstar has named](/guides/gta-6-map-leonida/).

## Why the 1980s still show up

GTA 6 is set in the present, but it keeps nodding to the old city. The Vintage Vice City Pack, a pre-order bonus, includes retro outfits and a '55 Vapid Stanier ([bonus details](/guides/gta-6-pre-order-bonuses/)). That is a deliberate callback, a bridge between the 1986 original and the modern game.

## Can you still play the original?

The 2002 game was remastered in Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition (2021), which has had a mixed reception. Availability differs by platform and store, so check yours before planning around it. You do not need to play it before GTA 6: the new game has new characters and a new story.

## What's carried over

- **The name and the Miami inspiration.** Beaches, Art Deco-style streets and a coastal skyline are still at the core.
- **Tone.** Satire of American excess, aimed now at the present instead of the 1980s.

## What's new

- **Two protagonists** instead of one.
- **A whole state**, not just a city.
- **Current-generation hardware only**, which lets the city be far busier and more detailed than any earlier version.
