**GTA 6 returns to Vice City for the first time in a mainline entry since 2002, transitioning the iconic fictional Miami setting from a 1980s period piece into a contemporary satirical metropolis.** While the original *Grand Theft Auto: Vice City* captured the pastel neon, synth-pop, and drug-trade cinema of 1986, Grand Theft Auto VI reimagines the city within a vast, modern state—Leonida—reflecting twenty-four years of hardware evolution, expanded biomes, and social media culture.

While Vice City debuted in the top-down 1997 original as one of three selectable metropolitan hubs alongside Liberty City and San Andreas, the setting established its cultural identity with the 2002 release on PlayStation 2.

## 2002 vs 2026: Setting, Culture, and Tone

The 2002 classic was unapologetically defined by 1980s pop culture: *Miami Vice*, *Scarface*, pastel suits, roller-skaters on Ocean Drive, and one of the most celebrated licensed soundtracks in gaming history across radio stations like Flash FM and Wave 103. Tommy Vercetti’s journey was a classic mafia climb from street-level enforcer to drug kingpin.

By contrast, Grand Theft Auto VI grounds Vice City in the present day. As showcased in [Rockstar's official reveal trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QdBZY2fkU-0), the cultural lens shifts toward contemporary Florida satire: viral livestreaming culture, social media accounts, influencer lifestyle excess, mud-bogging subcultures, and hyper-commercialized luxury condo developments. The protagonists, [Jason and Lucia](/guides/gta-6-jason-and-lucia/), reflect a modern Bonnie-and-Clyde dynamic navigating high-stakes robberies rather than a retro cartel syndicate.

## Map Scale, Biomes, and Architectural Density

The geographical scope represents the most drastic transformation between the two eras:

- **The 2002 Layout:** The PS2 map consisted of two primary landmasses separated by a central body of water: Vice City Beach to the east and the mainland to the west, connected by four bridges. The entire playable area spanned approximately 9 to 10 square kilometers. Hardware constraints meant nearly all building exteriors were flat textures, and water was impassable (Tommy Vercetti drowned instantly upon touching water).
- **GTA 6's State of Leonida:** Modern Vice City is not an isolated island; it serves as the metropolitan anchor of the larger State of Leonida. Confirmed regions include the urban sprawl of Vice City, the southern Keys archipelago, the murky Grassrivers wetlands (inspired by the Florida Everglades), and smaller industrial towns like Port Gellhorn. ## Hardware Leap: Emotion Engine to Modern Current-Gen

The leap from the PlayStation 2's 294 MHz Emotion Engine CPU and 32 MB of system RAM to the PS5 and Xbox Series X architecture fundamentally reshapes how open-world cities function.

In 2002, driving down Ocean Drive at high speed frequently resulted in "texture pop-in" where palm trees and luxury sports cars materialized meters in front of the vehicle. Fog effects and draw-distance limits were technical masks used to hide rendering bottlenecks. 

In GTA 6, high-speed NVMe SSD streaming (exceeding 5,500 MB/s raw bandwidth) allows draw distances that extend across the entire horizon, rendering distant skyscraper reflections and ocean waves seamlessly. Ray-traced reflections replace static environment maps on neon-lit streets, transforming nighttime rainstorms into complex optical simulations. ## Retro Callbacks: How the 1980s Survive in GTA 6

While GTA 6 is set firmly in the 2020s, Rockstar deliberately bridges the gap between old and new for longtime fans:

1. **Ocean Drive Architecture:** The distinctive Art Deco pastel facades of South Beach remain prominent landmarks, faithfully modeled with modern architectural weathering.
2. **The Vintage Vice City Pack:** Rockstar's official pre-order incentive includes retro-styled outfits for Jason and Lucia alongside a '55 Vapid Stanier, directly paying homage to the aesthetics of the 1980s release ([pre-order bonus breakdown](/guides/gta-6-pre-order-bonuses/)).
3. **Radio & Audio Cues:** Trailers prominently featured Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road," continuing Rockstar's tradition of anchoring Vice City's identity in evocative classic rock and synthesizer melodies.

## Can You Still Play the Original Vice City?

Players wanting to experience the original 2002 campaign before November 2026 have limited options:

- **Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition (2021):** Available across modern consoles and PC via [Rockstar Games Store](https://store.rockstargames.com/). While it incorporates updated lighting and modern controller layouts, it suffered criticism at launch for visual bugs and altered art direction.
- **Original PS2 / PC Discs:** Physical copies remain playable on original hardware or backwards-compatible early PS3 models, though digital stores delisted the original un-remastered releases when the Definitive Edition debuted.
- **Story Continuity:** You do not need to play the 2002 game to understand GTA 6. Rockstar's HD universe (introduced in GTA IV) maintains a separate storyline from the 3D universe, meaning Jason and Lucia’s narrative stands entirely on its own.
